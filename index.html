02 How every asset here is built

These are not marketing lines. They are the rules I check against before a package is allowed to be submitted.

Full C# source

No DLLs, ever. You can read every line, step through it in the debugger, and patch it yourself if you have to. Review is easier for Unity too.

Zero build cost

Editor-only assemblies. Nothing reaches your player build — not a byte, not a define, not a stray Resources folder.

Nothing changes without a click

No project setting is touched until you press Fix. Scene and project edits go through Undo; importer changes are backed up and restorable per asset.

No dependencies

UnityEngine, UnityEditor and Unity.Profiling. No NuGet, no third-party packages, no version conflicts with whatever you already have.

No telemetry, no network

The tools never phone home. Nothing about your project leaves your machine — which also means they work fine behind a corporate firewall.

A person answers

Support goes to my own inbox. I read everything and reply within a day — usually with a fix or a build, not a ticket number.

Compatibility promise. Unity 2021.3 LTS through Unity 6, Built-in and URP, Windows / macOS / Linux editors. A package that would break on one of those does not ship until it does not.

03 The studio is one developer

And that is the point — you always talk to the person who wrote the code.

Hi, I'm Oleksandr Burdenko

Senior Unity developer, thirteen years in, based in Bonn, Germany. Mobile, desktop, WebGL and AR — from casual titles with a million installs to award-winning AR work for the Royal Air Force, Fife Tourism and Allergan.

Every project ran into the same walls: a profiler that shows numbers but no answers, a build that mysteriously doubled in size, a method someone wired up in the Inspector three months ago that nobody can find. I kept writing little editor tools to get past those walls. Buralex Studio is me polishing them into something you can install.

Why this matters for the tools

These are not weekend experiments against a demo scene. They were built against production projects with real deadlines, real low-end Android devices, and real build size limits.

Gameplay systems, architecture and performance optimisation are my day job: profiling and optimising memory, rendering and load times for low-end iOS and Android, Addressables content delivery, CI/CD with automated builds, unit tests for core systems.
